# ClaimAssist — illustrative reflection (spoilers)

> **Human-authored teaching illustration, not an actual chatbot transcript or validated outcome.** All Harbor Mutual facts are fictional. This is one possible line of inquiry, not the correct answer.

**Initial purpose:** Reduce settlement delays and workloads. **Broader possible frame:** Help households recover from loss quickly, accurately, and with fair opportunities to understand and challenge decisions.

**Known within the fictional case:** The pilot used experienced adjusters, some made unofficial claimant calls, planned caseloads could increase, speed is rewarded, and escalations take time. Agreement with the model is not an independent accuracy measure.

**Plausible mechanism to investigate:** Larger caseloads + speed-based ranking + slow escalation could make a nominal human review step less meaningful. Concealed damage missed in photos could compound with less careful review. This **does not establish** that harm will happen; it suggests what to study.

**Perspectives to examine:** A displaced household needing both speed and correctness; a newer adjuster with fewer opportunities to develop independent judgment; an experienced adjuster fielding escalations; a manager accountable for volume; people with difficulty documenting or appealing an estimate.

**Potential inquiry and design options (not recommendations):** Observe real review behavior under realistic load; audit independent decision quality; compare outcomes and appeals across groups/regions where lawful and appropriate; improve escalation economics; bound the eligible claim types; test storm loads; clarify accountable owners and pause authority; co-design clearer communications and accessible recourse.

**Questions requiring evidence:** Which informal checks improved decisions? How representative was the pilot? What is the source and nature of regional differences? What does an accurate settlement mean when damage is concealed? How does claim volume influence review? Who can contest an outcome and how?

**Moral imagination challenge:** Why optimize only settlement time? Could AI support more informed adjusters, better uncertainty recognition, compassionate recovery services, or a non-AI change that serves the same end?

**Decision ownership:** The chatbot does not approve or reject rollout. Responsible people must decide acceptable exposure, boundaries, testing, accountability, and the authority to intervene.
